# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Open Evals contributors, maintainers, and community members are committed to providing a
welcoming, safe, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience level, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and help others participate.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's information without permission.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and community safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, sustained
disruption, deliberate intimidation, and publication of private information are not tolerated.
This applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently
ban participants whose behavior is harmful or inappropriate. Enforcement decisions will consider
context, impact, and whether harmful behavior is repeated.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer using a non-public contact method
listed on their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue that identifies the people involved.
Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and will recuse themselves when
they are involved in a report.
